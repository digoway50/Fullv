The Four Pillars of Fleex Garment

01

The Anatomical Sherwani Ban

Standard commercial kurta collars easily collapse or fray. Our 1.25" bandhgala collar is interfaced with genuine horsehair canvas, ensuring it retains its noble upright stance throughout humid weddings and long ceremonial days while staying soft against the neck.

02

Egyptian Giza & Normandy Flax

We source only extra-long staple Egyptian Giza cotton yarns blended with Normandy flax linen. The resulting 140 GSM weave breathes effortlessly, wicks moisture naturally, and becomes softer and more luxurious with every wash.

03

Heritage Salwar Drape

We reject modern mass-market shortcuts. Each salwar is cut with a generous 48-inch ghera circumference for classical subcontinental volume and unrestricted comfort, finished with an 8.5-inch quadruple-stitched paincha cuff.

04

Single-Artisan Tailoring

Instead of assembly line stitching, each Fleex Garment suit is entrusted to a single master tailor from initial chalk lines to the final buttonhole. This guarantees precision pattern alignment and unmatched longevity.
