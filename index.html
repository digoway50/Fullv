About Fleex Garment

The Traditional, Redefined: Re-engineering South Asian menswear for modern life.

Founded on the premise that traditional eastern menswear should never compromise on comfort or structural fit, Fleex Garment re-engineers classic Kurta-Shalwars with long-staple Egyptian cotton and pure Normandy linen.

Every garment is individually cut and inspected at our ateliers before dispatch in custom garment-care trunks with brass travel hangers.
